# MOR — exact sheet crops

These are pixel crops from MOR Complete Branding Kit Collage(1).png, a flattened 1536 × 1024 sheet. No design was regenerated, repainted, vectorized, upscaled, or background-removed.

## Limitations

All PNGs retain their original opaque backgrounds, including any pattern, texture, shadow, or rounded-corner surround already in the sheet. These are not transparent logos, SVGs, editable masters, full-size social templates, or print-ready sticker cut files. Small icons and merchandise previews are limited to the pixels available in the sheet. Color swatches are sampled crops, not newly generated flat-color assets. Typography reference does not include font files.

Labels and panel borders are excluded from asset crops; typography reference intentionally includes its font labels. The original source sheet is included for comparison. index.json records the exact source rectangle for every crop. Contact-sheet thumbnails are scaled only for review; asset PNGs retain native pixel dimensions.

## Sheet color labels

Primary background #0B1220; MOR Blue #0D6EFD; Accent #3DA5FF; Surface #E5E7EB. These are transcribed from the sheet; the rendered swatch pixels can differ.
