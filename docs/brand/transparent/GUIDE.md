# MOR — transparent logo variants

AI-assisted background-removal edits based on the exact sheet crops. PNGs have
real alpha. The app icon keeps its blue tile; only outside the rounded corners
is transparent.

These are not pixel-exact extractions or vector masters. Edges may be sharper
than the sheet, and some light artwork has dark outlines. Larger pixel sizes
do not imply print readiness.

Dashboard serves resized copies from `frontend/public/brand/` (see
`docs/mor-brand.md`). Use white / light marks on dark UI surfaces; use black
or blue monochrome on light surfaces.
